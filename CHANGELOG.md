# Rollwise

## 1.0.0
- Wishlist (per character): point at any item (AtlasLoot, auction house, chat, anywhere) and press the wishlist key (one-click "Use Ctrl+W", or any key in Keybindings), Shift-click item links while the Wishlist tab is open, Alt-click items you have, drag one onto the tab, or paste a link / item ID. When one drops, its card glows gold, a sound plays and a banner shows, even if only others can roll on it. Tracks times seen, when and where, and who got it; marks it "Got it" when you win it (or removes it, optional). Wishlist line on item tooltips, a star and a Wishlist filter in the Journal.
- Rollwise look (default): flat dark panels with a hairline border and soft shadow, an accent line in the item's quality colour, tier tags, roll pills, roll bars that fill in, a gold shine across the winner's line, and cards that slide in. Tooltip and Classic looks remain as options; animations can be turned off.
- Roll board: one card per item, picks grouped strongest first (Need main spec, Need off spec, Transmog, Greed) with a roll bar for each player. Main / off spec comes from the game's own roll state.
- The game's Need / Greed window docks on top of its card; moves and scales with the board, and goes back exactly where the game had it.
- Your odds: your chance to win from the picks so far.
- Gear check: item level against the piece you'd replace.
- Loot count bubble: items each player already won this run (hover for the list).
- Hidden-by-the-game markers instead of silently missing data.
- Recent tray: the last items that left the board; click one to bring its card back.
- Chat: hide your own choices, others' choices, roll numbers and/or results, with All / Results only / None presets, everywhere or only in dungeons and raids. Works in any client language (uses the game's own message templates).
- Journal: runs per dungeon or raid, grouped by boss, search and filters. Players tab with MS / OS wins and who has had nothing yet. Export as text, CSV or Discord.
- /rollwise preview: four scenarios that show the game's rules; /rollwise preview raid for a 25-player roll.
